# Support FAQ

## Refunds
Refunds are available within 30 days of purchase. Refunds are issued to
the original payment method and take 5-7 business days to appear.

## Shipping
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Expedited shipping takes
1-2 business days and is available at checkout for an extra fee.

## Account changes
Customers can update their email address and password from the account
settings page. Changing the account's registered country requires
contacting support directly, since it affects tax and shipping rules.

## Cancelling an order
Orders can be cancelled free of charge within 1 hour of placing them.
After that, the order may already be in fulfilment and a return will be
needed instead.
